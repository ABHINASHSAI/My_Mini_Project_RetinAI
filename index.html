About RetinAI

A production-grade CNN-based medical image classification system for preliminary eye disease screening. Built with transfer learning, explainable AI (Grad-CAM), and a full clinical insight module.

CNN Architecture

EfficientNetB0 / ResNet50 with transfer learning on ImageNet weights, fine-tuned on retinal datasets. Batch normalization, Dropout 0.4, early stopping, and LR scheduling.

Grad-CAM Explainability

Class Activation Mapping visualizes which retinal regions most influenced the prediction — enabling ophthalmologists to verify AI reasoning and build trust in outputs.

Medical Insight Module

Beyond classification — generates disease descriptions, probable causes, risk factors, evidence-based treatments, lifestyle precautions, and specialist referral recommendations.

Privacy & Security

HIPAA-aware design. All processing runs client-side in the browser — no images are transmitted to external servers. Local session-only patient history.
